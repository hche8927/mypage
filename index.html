Research Highlights

AMMNet: Asymmetric Multi-Modal Network for Earth Observation Segmentation

Asymmetric architecture fusing RGB with Digital Surface Model data — state-of-the-art on ISPRS Vaihingen & Potsdam at reduced cost. Neurocomputing 2026.

Skim Transformer for Light Field Super-resolution

Branches attend over skimmed subsets of sub-aperture images, disentangling disparity with fewer parameters. IEEE TMM 2026.

Sparse-View Splatting via Cascade Depth Loss

Hierarchical Depth-Guided Splatting (HDGS) with Cascade Pearson Correlation Loss — SOTA novel-view synthesis on LLFF & DTU. PhD core contribution. ICME 2026.

Fast 3D Splatting with Linear Kernels

3D Linear Splatting replaces the Gaussian fall-off with a bounded linear kernel — 30%+ faster real-time rendering with sharper detail. PhD core contribution. arXiv 2024, under review IEEE TVCG.

3D Reconstruction Using Neuromorphic Cameras

End-to-end voxel pipeline mapping raw event streams to occupancy without physical priors or known camera parameters, plus a released synthetic dataset. PhD core contribution. ICVR 2023, ICME 2025.
